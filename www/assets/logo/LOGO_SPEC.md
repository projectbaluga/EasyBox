# EasyBox Logo Specification

## Brand Colors
- **Primary:** Emerald Green `#2ECC71` (symbolizes "Success" / "Go/Online" status)
- **Primary Dark:** `#27AE60` (hover/depth variant)
- **Secondary:** Slate Dark Gray `#2C3E50` (contrast and text)
- **Secondary Light:** `#34495E` (subtle depth)
- **Accent Gray:** `#7F8C8D` (tagline/muted text)

## Logo Concept
- **Symbol:** A stylized open box with Wi-Fi signal radiating upward
- **Style:** Soft Geometric, flat vector design
- **Vibe:** "Friendly Utility" - easy to recognize at small sizes (like Grab or GCash icons)
